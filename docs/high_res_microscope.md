#High-resolution microscope

This is the standard research version of the OpenFlexure Microscope. It supports Standard RMS threaded objectives. It is a fully functioning motorised laboratory grade microscope. For more information on the microscope and its performance [see our paper in Optics Express](https://doi.org/10.1364/BOE.385729). If you use this microscope for research please consider citing this paper.

Before you start building the microscope you will need to source all the components listed our [bill of materials]{bom}.
